Ceiling water damage repair in Wisconsin Rapids starts with finding out what is above the ceiling. A roof leak through decking and insulation, an upstairs bathroom, a split supply line or an attic air handler can all produce the same brown ring. The source decides the repair, and the ceiling itself decides how urgent it is.

This page also owns roof leak cleanup, because the ceiling is where a roof leak shows up. Stains and damage on the walls beneath are handled on the wall and drywall page, and the drying work that follows is on the structural drying page.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling is an emergency when it is sagging, dripping steadily, bulging or sitting under a light fixture. Wet drywall and plaster get heavy, and a ceiling that has held water for hours can come down. Dispatch is open 24/7, so a drip at midnight is handled the same way as one at noon.

If the ceiling is dripping or bulging

  1. Move people and furniture out from under the wet spot. Do not stand beneath a sagging ceiling.
  2. Set a bucket under a drip. For a bulge, a small hole made with a screwdriver can release trapped water into a bucket, but only if you are not under the weakest area.
  3. Turn off the light and the circuit for that room. If water is near a fixture or the panel, call the utility or 911 about the electrical risk first.
  4. Photograph the ceiling, then call (715) 227-0967.

Signs of ceiling water damage

Brown or yellow rings

Rings that grow after each rain or each shower point to a repeating leak.

Sagging or bulging

Drywall holding water pulls away from the joists and bows downward.

Peeling or bubbling paint

Moisture behind the paint lifts it from the surface.

Dripping or a wet light

Water is moving through the ceiling cavity and collecting at a fixture.

Roof leak water damage, from the roof down

A roof leak rarely appears where the roofing failed. Water runs along sheathing and rafters, soaks insulation and finally shows up as a stain a few feet away.

In a Wisconsin winter, heat escaping into the attic can melt snow that refreezes at the eaves, and the water that backs up under shingles ends up in the ceiling. The Wisconsin insurance commissioner lists seepage behind drywall from an ice dam among the examples of water damage that a standard policy can cover.

A roofer stops the leak at the roof, and a restoration crew deals with the wet insulation, drywall and framing below. Wet attic insulation holds water against the ceiling for days, so it is removed and the cavity is dried. The crew checks the attic with a moisture meter and a thermal camera before any drywall goes back up.

How the ceiling is repaired

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    The source is traced and shut off or temporarily covered by the right trade.

  2. 2

    Open and remove

    Wet or sagging drywall and soaked insulation are cut out, usually in squares that expose the cavity.

  3. 3

    Dry the cavity

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing until moisture readings are normal.

  4. 4

    Patch and finish

    New drywall, texture and paint are installed, matched to the existing ceiling.

Repair or replace the ceiling?

A small stain on a dry ceiling can sometimes be sealed and painted. A ceiling that has sagged, held water or shown repeated staining should be opened, because wet insulation and rot stay behind a painted surface. Tell the crew if the area is textured or has a popcorn finish, because matching it takes extra steps.

What affects the price of ceiling repair

Area and height

Large or vaulted ceilings need more labor and access equipment.

Insulation involved

Wet attic insulation must be removed and replaced.

Finish type

Smooth, textured and popcorn ceilings need different patching and painting.

Source and rebuild

Fixing the roof or pipe is separate work, and the ceiling is priced once the cavity is dry.

Ceiling damage and insurance

Many policies cover sudden leaks and ice-dam seepage but exclude long-term neglect, so the cause matters. Photograph the stain and the attic, keep the roofer or plumber's report and note when you first saw the damage. Ask your insurer before permanent repairs start.

Ceiling water damage questions

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. Dispatch is open every hour, and a sagging or dripping ceiling is a good reason to call at any time.

Do you repair the roof?

The roof itself is a roofing job. Restoration covers the wet insulation, drywall and framing below it, and dispatch can tell you whether to call a roofer first.

Is it safe to leave a stained ceiling?

A dry, old stain is mostly cosmetic. A fresh or growing stain means water is still present and should be checked.

Why does the stain return after painting?

Paint hides the stain but not the moisture. If the source was not fixed or the cavity not dried, the stain comes through again.

How long does a ceiling repair take?

It depends on the size, the drying time and the finish. The crew gives you a schedule with the scope before work starts.
