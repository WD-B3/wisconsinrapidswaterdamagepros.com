Wet Mattress or Sofa After a Leak in Wisconsin Rapids

Mattresses and sofas hold water deep inside. Whether you can keep one depends on three things: the water type, the time and the material.

By the Wisconsin Rapids Water Damage Pros team · Published November 9, 2026

A wet mattress or sofa is one of the hardest decisions after a leak. The surface may look fine, but the foam and padding inside can hold gallons of water. This guide explains how to decide, starting with the type of water, since that is the single biggest factor.

The rules below are general, and a professional can test moisture and advise on a specific piece. If the water was sewage or flood water, the answer is usually to discard soft items.

Start with the water type

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard sorts water into three categories. Category 1 is clean water from a supply line or tank. Category 2 carries some contamination, like water from a washing machine or dishwasher. Category 3 is grossly contaminated, such as sewage or river flood water. Soft, porous furniture that touched category 3 water is normally thrown away.

How time changes the answer

Even with clean water, time matters. Padding and fabric start to grow mold when they stay damp for an extended period, and deeper foam can stay wet for days. A mattress that was soaked in clean water and dried quickly may be fine. One that sat wet for several days probably is not.

Check the smell. A musty odor that stays after drying means moisture remains inside, and it will likely return. Check the label as well. Memory foam, innerspring and latex dry differently, and some are harder to dry than others.

What to do right away

To give a wet mattress or sofa its best chance

  1. Stop the water source and get the piece out of the wet area as soon as it is safe to move.
  2. Stand it on its edge or lift it onto foil or plastic blocks so air reaches all sides.
  3. Take it outside to dry in sun and wind only if the weather is warm and dry, and bring it in before the evening damp.
  4. Photograph the piece and the water source so you have a record for your claim.

Mattress or sofa, the details

A mattress is thick foam, springs and fabric, and the middle stays wet longest. Pressing a towel into the center and checking for moisture tells you quickly. A sofa has a wood or metal frame, foam cushions and upholstery. Cushions can often be removed and dried separately, while the frame dries faster than the padding.

Leather and microfiber react differently. Leather can crack if it dries too fast, and microfiber can stain with water rings. Antique pieces, wood frames and special upholstery may be worth a professional opinion.

When furniture is covered by insurance

Contents coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the loss. The Wisconsin Office of the Commissioner of Insurance advises people to keep damaged items until the adjuster agrees. Photograph the piece before you move or discard it, and write down the brand and approximate age. If you must throw it away for health reasons, keep a photograph and a label or tag.

What this looks like in Wisconsin Rapids

Furniture damage often comes from a basement leak or a failed appliance. Basement flooding is covered on the basement water damage page, and appliance failures on the appliance leak page. Furniture stored in lower levels near the water is at the greatest risk.

Homes in Plover and Rudolph face the same exposure in a wet spring. Move soft furniture up off a basement floor before the thaw, or store it on shelves or pallets so a small seep does not soak it.

Cleaning a piece you decide to keep

If the water was clean and the piece dried quickly, vacuum the surface once it is fully dry, then sprinkle baking soda and vacuum again to take up odor. Spot clean stains with a mild upholstery cleaner and test it on a hidden area first. Do not use steam on foam, since it adds moisture.

Watch the piece over the next two weeks. If a musty smell returns, the core was not fully dry, and it is time to replace it. A mattress protector on a dried mattress helps, but it does not fix a damp interior.

Wet furniture questions

Should I move furniture out of my Wisconsin Rapids basement before spring?

If your home is near the river, yes. High groundwater can push water into basements, so store soft furniture up off the floor or on shelves.

Can a wet memory foam mattress be saved?

Rarely. Memory foam holds water deeply and dries slowly, so it is often replaced unless the water was clean and removed quickly.

Can I dry a sofa cushion in the sun?

Yes, if the water was clean and the weather is warm and dry. Turn it often and check the center.

How do I know mold has started?

A musty smell, dark spots or a sticky feel in the foam. If you see it, discard the piece.

Do restoration crews clean furniture?

The focus is the building. Ask the crew what can be moved and dried, and check your policy for contents.

What should I photograph for a claim?

The piece, the label, the water source and the room, before you move or discard anything.
