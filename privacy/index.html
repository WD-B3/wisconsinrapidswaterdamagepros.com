Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website or call our number.

Emergency Dispatch

Last updated November 9, 2026. Wisconsin Rapids Water Damage Pros operates this website and the dispatch phone line at (715) 227-0967.

Information we collect

This website does not have forms, and we do not ask you to type personal information into it. When you call, we may collect the phone number you call from, the address of the property and a description of the water damage so a crew can be arranged. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

Calls

Calls may be routed through a call-handling or call-tracking service. Calls may be recorded or logged for quality, training and attribution. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers.

Website data

Like most websites, the hosting service may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This website may use basic analytics to understand how pages are used. It does not use this data to identify you by name.

How information is used

We use call information to arrange a restoration crew, to respond to you and to improve our service. We may share the details you give us with the licensed and insured contractor who is sent to your property. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties and links

This website links to outside sources such as government and university sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Embedded maps are provided by Google and are subject to Google's policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email contact@wisconsinrapidswaterdamagepros.com or call (715) 227-0967. Our address is 2501 8th St S, Wisconsin Rapids, WI 54494.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
