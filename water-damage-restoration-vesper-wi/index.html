Vesper is a Wood County village of 513 people at ZIP code 54489, northwest of Wisconsin Rapids. Many of its buildings date from when the village was first platted, and that age is the main thing to know about water damage here. Wisconsin Rapids Water Damage Pros dispatches to Vesper around the clock for leaks, floods and backups.

A village shaped by a sawmill and a fire

Settlement began in the early 1850s, and Girard and Drake built a sawmill on Hemlock Creek in the 1870s. A post office opened in 1878. A wildfire in 1894 burned the mill and 23 houses, and the village was platted in 1897. A two-room brick school followed in 1906 and the State Bank of Vesper in 1911. Vesper was incorporated as a village in 1948 and takes its name from the vesper sparrows native to the area.

That timeline matters for a restoration crew. A home built in the years right after the 1897 plat is now well over a century old, and houses of that age often have original framing, old plaster, mixed plumbing and additions that were added over many decades. Hemlock Creek is the local water feature, and low spots near a creek hold water after a thaw.

When water is spreading in Vesper

A leak in a century-old house can travel behind plaster and out of sight, so waiting to see how bad it is can cost you. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, every day, and the person on the line will talk through the first steps. A crew is sent from Wisconsin Rapids once the situation is understood.

Before we arrive

Shut off the water

Close the main valve. Older homes may have a stiff valve, so find it before you need it.

Watch the ceilings

Plaster ceilings that hold water get heavy, so move out from under a stain.

Keep power away from water

If water is near wiring, call the utility or 911 about the risk first.

Photograph everything

Take photos of stains, the waterline and any items you move.
