Plover is the largest of the five nearby places dispatch covers, a village of 13,519 people at the 2020 census, ZIP code 54467. It is in Portage County, not Wood County, and it is a growing community with newer subdivisions, shops and a river on its western edge. Wisconsin Rapids Water Damage Pros dispatches to Plover around the clock.

Neighborhoods, parks and the two rivers

Plover is bisected by the Little Plover River, a spring-fed trout stream, and the Wisconsin River forms the village's western boundary. Galecke County Park gives boaters access to the river. Lake Pacawa is a 36-acre man-made lake with a Korean War Veterans Memorial, Standing Rocks County Park has mountain biking and ski trails, and the Green Circle Trail and Tomorrow River State Trail pass through.

Homes near the river and the creek have different water concerns from homes in newer subdivisions farther in.

The village has a long and unusual history. It was first incorporated in 1857, selected as Portage County's first county seat in 1844, lost that role to Stevens Point in 1868, and has since been incorporated more than once. The current village incorporation dates to 1971. For homeowners, the useful point is that Plover has both older areas and a lot of recent construction.

What newer homes in Plover tend to have

Newer subdivisions bring a different set of problems. Finished basements with carpet and drywall, laundry rooms on upper floors, ice makers and dishwashers, and air handlers in attics all create leak paths that are easy to overlook. Pressure-fed supply lines with crimp fittings and flexible hoses can fail without warning. The sources are covered on appliance leak water damage, AC leak water damage and kitchen water damage restoration.
