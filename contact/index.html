Call is the best way to reach us

Water damage gets worse by the hour, so we handle requests by phone. The person who answers will ask where the water came from, how far it has spread and whether electricity or sewage is involved. Then they will tell you what to do while a crew is arranged. We do not use online forms.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address of the property and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water started and whether it has been stopped.
  • Whether the water looks clean, dirty or like sewage.
  • Whether water is near outlets, the panel or a sagging ceiling.
  • The name of your insurer, if you have it, so you can report the loss the same day.

Questions about reaching us

Do you answer at night or on weekends?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays.

Can I email instead?

You can email contact@wisconsinrapidswaterdamagepros.com, but phone is much faster for an active leak.
