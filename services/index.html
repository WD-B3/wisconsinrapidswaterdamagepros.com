Every water loss follows the same basic order: stop the source, remove the water, take out what cannot be dried, dry the structure and rebuild. What differs is the source, the materials and the speed at which things get worse. The pages below are grouped by where the water comes from and what it touched, so you can find the one that fits.

If you are not sure which page applies, call the dispatch line and describe what you see. The person who answers will tell you what to do first and which kind of crew to send. The main water damage restoration page gives the overview, and the service area page lists the towns we dispatch to.

How to choose the right service page

Start with the source. If water came from a pipe, appliance or tank, the pipes and appliances groups apply. If it came from outside, like a river, rain or a storm, use the weather group. If you already know what it soaked, such as carpet, hardwood or drywall, the floors and walls pages explain what can be saved.

  1. 1

    Find the source

    A pipe, an appliance, a roof, the ground or a drain.

  2. 2

    Identify what it touched

    Carpet, hardwood, drywall, a ceiling, a basement or a commercial space.

  3. 3

    Check the safety issues

    Electricity, sewage and ceilings that sag come first.

  4. 4

    Call dispatch

    Describe it and get the first steps before a crew arrives.

Questions about our services

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Call and describe the water.

Which service page is right for me?

Match the source of the water first, then what it touched. If you are unsure, call dispatch.

Do you only do emergency work?

No. After drying, the same job can continue with repairs, or you can use another contractor for the rebuild.

Do you serve areas outside Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. Dispatch covers Port Edwards, Rudolph, Nekoosa, Vesper and Plover, and will tell you if another address is within reach.

How is the price set?

By the water category, the area, the materials, the drying time and the rebuild scope. The scope and price are explained before work starts.
