Slab leak water damage in Wisconsin Rapids involves a supply line that has failed in or under a concrete slab. The slab can be a garage, an addition, a slab-on-grade room or a basement floor with lines in it. Because the leak is out of sight, it can run for a long time and soak the subfloor, flooring and walls before anyone sees a sign.

A leak you can see under a sink is a different job, and it is on kitchen water damage or burst pipe water damage. The drying work that follows a slab leak is on the structural drying page, and a leak into a lower level is on basement water damage cleanup.

A slab leak is an emergency

A slab leak runs constantly, so the water bill and the moisture keep growing. Water that reaches flooring and walls from below can wick up a wall, and standing moisture under a slab can undermine a floor. Dispatch is open 24/7, including weekends.

If you suspect a slab leak

  1. Shut off the main water valve if you hear running water with all fixtures off.
  2. Stay off wet floors and keep away from any outlets in a damp room.
  3. Check the water meter. If it moves with everything off, a line is leaking.
  4. Call (715) 227-0967 and describe the signs.

Signs of a slab leak

Warm spots on the floor

A hot water line leaking beneath can warm a patch of the floor.

The sound of running water

A hiss or trickle when everything is off.

A rising water bill

A jump with no change in use.

Damp carpet, cracks or musty odor

Moisture pushing up through the floor.

How a slab leak is found and dried

A leak detection specialist or plumber pinpoints the line with acoustic and thermal tools, and the line is repaired or rerouted. Restoration handles what the water did: moisture mapping of the flooring and walls, extraction where water has pooled, and drying of the materials that were soaked from below.

  1. 1

    Confirm the leak

    The meter test and detection tools find the line.

  2. 2

    Stop the water

    The line is repaired or rerouted by a plumber.

  3. 3

    Map and dry

    Moisture meters and thermal cameras guide the drying of floors and walls.

  4. 4

    Repair

    Flooring, baseboards and drywall are repaired after the structure is dry.

Concrete holds moisture and releases it slowly, so drying a slab takes patience. The crew places dehumidifiers and air movers and checks the slab and the flooring on each visit. If a floor covering was glued down, it may need to be lifted so the slab can dry.

Slab leaks and insurance

Coverage for the leak and the damage often depends on the cause and how long the leak ran. Policies can treat the cost of reaching the pipe differently from the water damage. Keep the plumber's report, the meter readings and photos, and call your agent early.

Slab leak questions

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24 hours a day. Shut off the main valve if water is running, then call.

How do I know it is under the slab?

A moving meter with everything off, warm floor spots and the sound of running water point there. A specialist confirms it.

Does restoration find the leak?

A plumber or leak detection specialist finds and fixes the line. Restoration dries and repairs the damage.

Can the slab be dried?

Yes, with dehumidifiers and air movers, but it takes time, and the crew uses readings to know when it is done.

Is mold a concern?

It can be if moisture stays. Drying and ventilation lower the risk.
