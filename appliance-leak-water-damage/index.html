Appliance leak water damage in Wisconsin Rapids covers washing machine hose failures, dishwasher leaks, refrigerator ice maker lines, and overflows from other water-connected appliances. These losses are common because the appliances run unattended and the hoses wear out. A small leak can spread across a floor and into the room below.

This page is about the appliance as the source. Kitchen cabinets and flooring are on kitchen water damage restoration, water heaters are on water heater leak cleanup, and an air conditioner is on AC leak water damage.

An appliance leak is an emergency

A hose that lets go can pour water for as long as it is connected, and a washer can empty its drum onto the floor. Dispatch is open every hour of every day, so call as soon as the supply is off.

If an appliance is leaking

  1. Shut off the supply valve behind or under the appliance, or the main valve if you cannot reach it.
  2. Unplug the appliance only if the floor and outlet are dry. If water is near the outlet or panel, call the utility or 911 about the electrical risk.
  3. Pull the appliance away from the wall only if it is safe, to see the source and the floor behind it.
  4. Photograph the appliance and the water, then call (715) 227-0967.

Signs of an appliance leak

Water around the unit

Puddles at the base, or damp spots behind it.

Swollen or stained floor

Flooring that lifts or darkens near the appliance.

A musty smell

Odor from a laundry room, kitchen or closet.

A stain on the ceiling below

Water from an upstairs unit moving through the floor.

Water from a washing machine hose is usually clean at first, but a drain overflow carries soap and lint. A dishwasher releases gray water. The water category affects whether carpet and porous materials can be dried or must be removed, as explained on the carpet water damage page.

How cleanup works

  1. 1

    Stop and extract

    The supply is shut off, and standing water is removed.

  2. 2

    Move the appliance

    The unit is pulled out so the floor and wall base can be checked.

  3. 3

    Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the floor, walls and any room below.

  4. 4

    Repair

    Flooring, baseboards and drywall are repaired or replaced.

A leak behind or under an appliance is easy to miss. The crew checks behind the unit, under the floor and into the ceiling of the room below, using moisture meters. The drying approach is explained on the structural drying page.

Appliance leaks and insurance

Insurers often cover sudden appliance failures, but they may treat a slow leak, wear and the cost of the appliance itself differently. Keep the failed hose or part, photograph the scene and ask your agent what is covered before you replace flooring.

Appliance leak questions

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day. Shut off the supply and call.

Do you repair the appliance?

No. An appliance technician repairs or replaces it. Restoration handles the water and the damage.

Can a washer hose really cause that much damage?

Yes. A burst hose can run for as long as it is connected, and the water spreads through floors.

Should I replace old hoses?

Many people replace rubber hoses with braided lines and check them yearly. Ask the appliance maker for guidance.

What if the leak was behind the unit for weeks?

That needs a moisture check of the floor and walls, and the damage may be deeper than it looks.
