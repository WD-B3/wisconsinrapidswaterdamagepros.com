Storm water damage cleanup in Wisconsin Rapids covers water that entered during heavy rain, wind, melting snow or ice. It may come through a roof, a window, a door, a vent or a foundation wall. Storm water often carries dirt and debris, so it is treated carefully.

This page is about storm entry points. Rising rivers and groundwater are on flood damage cleanup, roof leak damage to ceilings is on ceiling water damage repair, and basement seepage is on basement water damage cleanup.

Storm water in the house is an emergency

Storm damage often arrives with power outages and blocked roads, so calling early matters. Dispatch is open 24/7, and the person on the line can talk you through the first steps. In a large storm many homes need help at once, and dispatch explains the situation honestly.

If storm water is inside

  1. Stay away from downed lines, standing water near outlets and areas with a sagging ceiling.
  2. Move people and belongings out of the wet area, and cover openings if it is safe, using a tarp on an interior side or plastic sheeting.
  3. Do not climb on a wet or icy roof. Call a roofer for the opening.
  4. Photograph the damage, then call (715) 227-0967.

Signs of storm entry

Wet ceilings and walls

Stains or dripping after a storm, especially near windows or the roofline.

Wet window frames and sills

Water that blew through gaps or failed seals.

Water at doors

Pooling at thresholds and garage doors.

Damp insulation and attic

Moisture around vents, chimneys and flashing.

Heavy rain in Wood County

The State Climatology Office lists a Wood County record of 5.17 inches of rain in one day, at Babcock on September 22, 2016. A storm that drops that much rain can overwhelm gutters, sump pumps and roof drainage in a short time. Even smaller storms push water sideways in strong wind, and a roof that is fine in a normal rain can leak in a driving one.

Storm damage and insurance

Wind-driven rain through a damaged roof or window is often covered, while surface flooding is commonly excluded. Photograph the opening and the interior, keep receipts for temporary protection and ask your insurer before making permanent repairs.

Storm water damage questions

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Wisconsin Rapids?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered every hour of every day, though large storms can mean many calls at once.

Do you fix the roof?

A roofer repairs the opening. Restoration handles the water and the damage inside.

Is storm water clean?

Often not. It can carry dirt and debris, so crews treat it with care.

Should I cover the opening myself?

Only if it is safe. Never climb on a wet or icy roof.

How long should I wait to file a claim?

Call your insurer promptly and keep records of what you did to limit the damage.
